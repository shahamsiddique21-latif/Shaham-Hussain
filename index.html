03. Narrative

Story & 6 Chapters

CHAPTER 01

Childhood Separation

Manzoor gets lost from his parents as a young boy and grows up to become an undefeated commander.

CHAPTER 02

The Emperor's Mission

The King commands Manzoor to take 45 soldiers and hunt down the scar-faced rebel Suleiman Khan.

CHAPTER 03

Night Attack & Faith

Rival bandits ambush the camp, but Manzoor unites his troops with faith and prevails.

CHAPTER 04

Recovery in Vestro

Injured in battle, Manzoor is brought to Vestro city where he receives urgent medical care.

CHAPTER 05

Reunion with Shaham & Mudassir

Manzoor meets his childhood friend Shaham and reunites with his younger brother Mudassir at the blacksmith forge.

CHAPTER 06

March to Rectos Air

Discovering Suleiman Khan is raiding Rectos Air village where his parents reside, Manzoor rides to save them.

09. Technical Insights

Challenges & Solutions

Facial Consistency

Challenge: Maintaining character face features for Manzoor and Shaham across different scenes.

Solution: Used custom prompt embeddings and fixed image-to-image reference controls.

Action Fluidity

Challenge: Simulating fast-paced horse riding and sword combat.

Solution: Frame interpolation and multi-layer motion brush rendering.
